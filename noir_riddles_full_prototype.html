A Trail of Ink

The library smells of old paper and older knowledge. You find Elena's research desk in the linguistics section. A coffee cup, half-full and cold. A German newspaper lies open.

A bookmark falls out as you flip through a volume on Indo-European roots. On it, scrawled in hurried handwriting:

A librarian approaches. "That young woman came here often. Always met someone at the café across the street. Strange pair—she'd speak German with him, but their notes..." She trails off. "Full of symbols."

:: UNIVERSITY ARCHIVE ::

The Last Page

The archive is a maze of shelves and shadows. Dust motes dance in the dim light. And there, on a reading desk, lies an open manuscript.

Elena's final work. The Babel Manuscript. Pages of meticulous research tracing Greek, German, English, Spanish, and even Russian words back to their Proto-Indo-European roots.

And a note, in her hand:

"They killed me for this. The connections are real. Greek patér, German Vater, English father, Spanish padre, Russian отец (otets)... all from PIE *pəter*. The first society... they'll stop at nothing to keep this hidden."

A floorboard creaks behind you.

"Ah. I see you've found it." A man steps from the shadows. Tall. Speaking with a Russian accent. "Dr. Volkov, at your service. Though Elena would have said sluzhba."

He holds a gun. "Unfortunately, this manuscript belongs to my employers. The Babel Society doesn't take kindly to... outsiders."

:: THE LAST WORD ::

Epilogue

The confrontation is brief. A struggle. A gunshot. Volkov lies bleeding on the archive floor, the manuscript clutched in your hands.

As the police arrive, you slip the pages into your coat. The Babel Society will come looking. But for now...

You've solved the case. And learned something deeper: All our words are connected.
